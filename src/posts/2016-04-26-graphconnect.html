During Neo4j’s GraphConnect Europe 2016 conference I presented the proposed recommender systems of Part-up. This project is also my graduation research at Delft University of Technology. I designed and developed a recommender system with collaborative filtering algorithms. Running on a Neo4j database and GraphAware engine. The presentation included the architecture, collaborative filtering algorithms and user insights.
